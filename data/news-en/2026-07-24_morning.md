## 1. Market themes / 市场主线

This morning is less about a mechanical rebound after Thursday's selloff and more about the sequencing of three pressures. First, oil is backing away from the $100-plus shock and relieving inflation stress. Second, the long end of the Treasury curve remains high enough to keep valuation discipline in place. Third, the post-earnings reaction to Alphabet and Tesla has made investors demand clearer evidence that AI and autonomy spending can convert into cash flow.

That combination argues for a cautious stabilization frame. If oil continues lower and yields follow, the market can shift from stress repair to normal earnings digestion. If oil merely bounces around while the 10-year yield stays close to 4.7%, rallies in long-duration growth are more likely to be sold. AI is splitting into two trades. One is Intel-style demand evidence, where earnings can still be rewarded if revenue acceleration looks real. The other is Alphabet/Tesla-style capex skepticism, where investors push back when investment intensity outruns near-term cash-flow proof. The market is no longer buying the label automatically; it is demanding conversion from spending to revenue to cash flow.
